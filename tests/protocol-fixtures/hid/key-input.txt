# LCD key input on VLP index 2 (spec §5.6), interleaved with the two image
# fragment ACKs and the display mode-change event that share the index.
# Expected: press 5, press 1, release 5, release 1; the ACKs and the event
# yield nothing.
#
# The hcooper captures carry no key input. The key reports follow the
# layout logilinux and Julusian decode (`13 ff 02 00 <seq> 01 <keys> 00`,
# docs/research/logilinux-and-julusian-analysis.md §4.6, table row 22);
# their sequence bytes are made up until the hardware check (plan §5
# item 13). The ACKs are frames #1782 and #1785, the event frame #1770 of
# hcooper 03-options-plus-startup.pcap (MIT, see README.md).
#
# 1. Key 5 held.
< 13 ff 02 00 c0 01 05 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
# 2. ACK of fragment 0 (#1782).
< 13 ff 02 2e c0 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
# 3. Keys 5 and 1 held.
< 13 ff 02 00 c1 01 05 01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
# 4. ACK of fragment 1 (#1785): `c1 00 01`, the "echo as key 1" of other
#    projects.
< 13 ff 02 2e c1 00 01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
# 5. Display mode-change event, mode a0 (#1770).
< 13 ff 02 10 c6 a0 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
# 6. Key 1 held only.
< 13 ff 02 00 c2 01 01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
# 7. Nothing held.
