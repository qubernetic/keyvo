# The Dialpad search while the Dialpad is in deep sleep: slots 1 and 2 hold
# paired devices without 0x4610, the receiver answers connect fail (0x04)
# for slot 3 at once, and resource error (0x09) for the empty slots 4 to 6.
# The search finds no Dialpad. Later the Dialpad wakes and announces it on
# its own device index: a 0x1D4B reconnect report and a 0x1004 battery
# report. The receiver sends no 0x41 arrival; the next search round finds
# the Dialpad (receiver-slot-probe.txt).
#
# Source: own, the owner's hardware checks of slice 7 (#187), Dialpad
# firmware RBO 07.00.B0016:
# - slots 1 and 2 and step 4: part 2 (2026-10-30,
#   https://github.com/qubernetic/keyvo/issues/187#issuecomment-6517302440),
#   `keyvo --dump dial` at 8f56281, block "wake, search, diverts" (ms
#   32611 to 33397); step 4 is the first two frames of that block;
# - slot 3: part 2's connect fail of the overnight-sleeping Dialpad,
#   `10 03 8f 00 0f 04 00`;
# - slots 4 to 6: part 1 (2026-10-29,
#   https://github.com/qubernetic/keyvo/issues/187#issuecomment-6504351636),
#   the error replies of the empty slots at 1530129, before the fix of
#   #200; their software id is 0xf here instead of that build's 0xe, as the
#   receiver echoes the request's.
#
